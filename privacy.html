Privacy Policy

Karaoke — https://mjhalim777.github.io/karaoke/ · Last updated: November 28, 2026

Karaoke is a personal, non-commercial home karaoke website. A TV shows a song queue and plays karaoke videos; guests use their phones to search for songs and add them to the queue.

YouTube API Services

This website uses YouTube API Services to search for videos and the YouTube embedded player to play them. By using this website you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube and Google process data according to the Google Privacy Policy.

What information is used

  • Search words you type are sent to the YouTube Data API to find matching videos. They are not stored by this website.
  • The song queue for a room: video ID, video title, and the singer name you choose to type (optional; any nickname works). This is kept in Google Firebase Realtime Database only so the TV and phones in the same room can share the queue.
  • Usage counts: the number of searches per day and how many pages are connected, with no personal information, to show how much of the free limits is used.
  • Your device remembers your last room code and singer name in its own browser storage, so you don't have to type them again. You can clear this in your browser settings.

What is not collected

No accounts, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, or payment details are collected. No YouTube data is sold or shared, and videos are never downloaded or modified; they play only through YouTube's official embedded player.

Retention and deletion

Songs leave the queue as soon as they are played or removed. The site owner deletes old rooms, including any stored video titles and singer names, at least every 30 days. To have room data deleted sooner, contact the site owner through the project's GitHub page. You can also revoke this site's access to your data through the Google security settings page.

Cookies

This website does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies. YouTube's embedded player and Google services may set cookies as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
